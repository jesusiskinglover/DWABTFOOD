About DW ABT FOOD

DW ABT FOOD is a website designed to help people figure out a way to not worry about rising food costs and/or food insecurity and instead get the help they need. If you are in any need of food, dont worry about it. At DW ABT FOOD, we help you not worry about food. Right now, you can find different links to different food drives in the DMV. Any links for food drives outisde the DMV are coming soon.

About Food Insecurity

Food insecurity is a crititcal problem and affects more than 48 million people in the US alone. While I can't fight food insecurity entirely, I hope you can help me sustain this website and/or donate to food drives to help out.

The different types of food insecurity

According to the image, there are 4 types of food insecurity:
High Food Insecurity: Marginal Food Insecurity: Low Food Insecurity: Very Low Food Insecurity:
High food insecurity is when people dont have any problems related to getting food Marginal food insecurity is when people have some troubles getting food Low food is when people reduce the quality desire and variety of food. This doent really impact eating problems or food intake though Very low food insecurity is when a person lacked money for food, which disrupted eating patterns

Why I picked this topic

I picked this topic because I sometimes go volunteer at a food drive and see a lot of older people who need food (or people in general)
